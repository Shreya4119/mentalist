How this score works

The AI only reads figures from the report. Mentalist then computes each ratio with a fixed formula and rates it Healthy (100), Caution (60) or Concern (20) against general rule-of-thumb ranges. Each area's score is the average of its ratios. The overall score weights Profitability 25%, Liquidity 20%, Leverage 20%, Cash flow 20%, Growth 10% and Efficiency 5%, over the areas that have data. Bands: Strong 75+, Stable 55–74, Watch 35–54, Weak below 35. Healthy ranges differ by industry, so treat this as a starting point, not a verdict.
