About & privacy

Mentalist is a learning project: an AI mentor for reading company annual reports and getting started with investing.

What it is, and what it isn’t

  • It reads annual reports with an AI model, computes standard ratios and a health score in code, and explains them in plain language.
  • It is educational, not financial, tax or legal advice. It doesn’t know your situation and can misread a report. Check important figures in the original document, and talk to a licensed adviser before big decisions.
  • A healthy company isn’t automatically a good investment: the share price may already be too high.

Your data

  • Your account is just a username and a password (stored as a secure hash). No email, no real name.
  • Reports you upload are private: only you can see them. Sample reports added by the site owner are visible to everyone.
  • To analyse a report, the relevant pages are sent to the AI provider (Google Gemini). Questions you ask are sent there too. Upload public annual reports, not personal documents.
  • Household health checks you save are stored with your account so you can come back to them. Rounded numbers are fine.
  • Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to stop abuse (too many sign-in attempts or new accounts). It isn’t stored in the database.
  • No tracking: no ads, no analytics, no cookies. Your sign-in and theme are kept in your own browser’s storage.
  • Delete everything any time from Your account: your account, your reports and their files, and your health checks are removed permanently.

Run it yourself

The full source is on GitHub (Spring Boot, Angular, Postgres + pgvector). Run it on your own computer with your own AI key to analyse any report. Questions or problems: open an issue there.
