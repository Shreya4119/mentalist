Companies

Type the name of a company listed on an EU stock market and press Analyse. Mentalist finds its official annual filings itself, reads several years and shows the trends and warning signs. No upload needed, and every number comes straight from the filing.

Works for companies listed on an EU-regulated market (annual reports from 2020–2021 onwards).
